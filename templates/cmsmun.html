A forum for young diplomats, debaters and global citizens. The CMS Model United Nations conference brought delegates together for research, structured debate, negotiation and dialogue on international issues.

24–25 April 2026CMS Aliganj Campus ICOMPLETED · ARCHIVE

THE CONFERENCE

Diplomacy in action.

The brochure describes CMSMUN as a platform for students from across the country and beyond to engage with global perspectives, debate real-world issues and work towards consensus. It presents the conference as a learning experience centred on critical thinking, diplomacy, cooperation and responsible global citizenship.

COMMITTEES & FORUMS

Ten rooms. Global questions.

01 · PARLIAMENTARY DEBATE

Lok Sabha

India’s approach to regulation and oversight in hate speech and media trials, alongside the question of states’ autonomy within India’s federal structure.

02 · UNITED NATIONS

United Nations General Assembly (UNGA)

Strengthening and advancing the global framework for arms control, disarmament and non-proliferation treaties.

03 · UNITED NATIONS

United Nations Security Council (UNSC)

The situation in Central Africa, reviewing regional strategy to combat the Lord’s Resistance Army and the repatriation of former combatants.

04 · REGIONAL ORGANISATION

Organization of American States (OAS)

Strengthening multilateral security cooperation to combat transnational narcotics networks and organised crime in Latin America.

05 · UNITED STATES

U.S. House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform

Legislative reform and intelligence accountability following the 2013 NSA surveillance disclosures.

06 · UNITED NATIONS

International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA)

Long-term international safety oversight at the Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant, with emphasis on the New Safe Confinement.

07 · CRISIS SIMULATION

Emergency Council of Advisors — Republic of Peru

A simulated emergency session on the aftermath of the Moquegua explosion and escalating geopolitical tension. The brochure sets the freeze date as 3 May 2043.

08 · UNITED NATIONS

Committee on the Peaceful Uses of Outer Space (COPUOS)

Reviewing and advancing the Space 2030 Agenda for the sustainable and equitable use of outer space.

09 · INTERNATIONAL COURT

International Court of Justice (ICJ)

Jadhav (India v. Pakistan), General List No. 168. The brochure gives a freeze date of 8 May 2017.

10 · MEDIA & REPORTING

International Press

Journalism, photography and caricature, documenting debate and conference proceedings for the wider public.

BROCHURE INFORMATION

Delegate guidelines.

Eligibility & delegation

Grades 8–12; the brochure states ages 13–19. Each participating school was asked to send a delegation of at least eight students.

Fees & accommodation

The brochure lists INR 2,700 per participant for Lucknow delegates and outstation delegates without accommodation, and INR 3,500 for outstation delegates opting for accommodation. It says accommodation included meals on both conference days and dinner at the CISV/KAIZEN Guest House, CMS Kanpur Road.

CMSMUN 2026 has concluded. Registration links, fee amounts and participation rules shown here are historical details transcribed from the brochure; they are not confirmation that registration is currently open.
