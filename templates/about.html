CAMPUS LEADERSHIP

Principal's Desk

Under the leadership of Principal Mrs Shivani Singh, CMS Aliganj Campus I places emphasis on academic growth, curiosity, creativity and compassion. The campus message highlights the 6 Cs — Critical Thinking, Creativity, Collaboration, Communication, Character and Citizenship — alongside the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

The campus approach combines technology-enhanced classrooms with inquiry-based and experiential learning. Cambridge learning has expanded through Grade IX, while the Design & Technology Lab, Maker Space and Robotics & Artificial Intelligence facilities give students opportunities to explore ideas through practical work.

Learning also extends beyond lessons through literature, environmental initiatives, sports, cultural activities and opportunities for service. The aim is to help students become independent learners, thoughtful problem-solvers and responsible global citizens.

Profile adapted from the official CMS Aliganj Campus I Principal’s Desk and campus profile.

03 · WHOLE-CHILD LEARNING

Child Development

The campus describes a child-centred approach that combines academic learning with sports, music, fine arts, literature, environmental initiatives and collaborative activities. Its stated educational priorities include the 6 Cs: critical thinking, creativity, collaboration, communication, character and citizenship.

Initiatives described by CMS include the Junior Youth Empowerment Programme and “Each One–Teach One”, alongside student participation in community service and environmental responsibility projects.

04 · VALUES IN ACTION

Education Impact

CMS frames education around universal values, global understanding, excellence and service to the world. Its school-wide work includes peace education, interfaith understanding, global citizenship and social action. CMS reports receiving the UNESCO Prize for Peace Education in 2002.

At Aliganj Campus I, official campus information describes outreach around water and energy conservation, animal care, social service and peer mentoring. These activities connect classroom learning with responsibility in the wider community.

05 · THE FOUNDING VISION

Our Founders

City Montessori School was founded in Lucknow in 1959 by Dr Jagdish Gandhi and Dr Bharti Gandhi, beginning with five children. Inspired by the ideals of Mahatma Gandhi and Sant Vinoba Bhave, the founders emphasised peace, human unity, values-based education and service to humanity.

The motto “Jai Jagat” expresses this universal outlook. Their vision continues in CMS’s emphasis on “Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam” — the world is one family — and on preparing students to contribute thoughtfully to society.

06 · PEOPLE WHO SUPPORT LEARNING

CMS Management

CMS’s published leadership information includes its central management, campus principals, senior principals, teachers, counsellors and administrative teams. At Aliganj Campus I, the official campus page identifies Mrs Shivani Singh as Principal and Ms Lovey Khan as Vice-Principal.

Campus councils and teaching teams support students across Pre-Primary, Primary, Junior, Senior and Cambridge sections.
